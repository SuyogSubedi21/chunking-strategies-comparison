Himalayan Sample Bank recorded a net profit of NPR 4.2 billion in fiscal year 2023/24. Net profit grew by 12 percent compared with the previous year. Operating income rose to NPR 11.8 billion, helped by higher interest income.

Total deposits reached NPR 312 billion at the end of the year. Savings deposits from retail customers made up most of the growth. Fixed deposits accounted for 40 percent of total deposits. Deposit growth was strongest in rural branches.

Loans and advances stood at NPR 268 billion. Home loans grew fastest, rising by 18 percent. Lending to small businesses also increased. The non-performing loan ratio fell to 2.7 percent, below the regulatory limit.

The capital adequacy ratio was 13.1 percent, comfortably above the 11 percent minimum required by Nepal Rastra Bank. The bank raised fresh capital through a rights share issue in the second quarter.

Mobile banking users crossed two million during the year. Payments through the mobile wallet doubled. Customers now complete most fund transfers through the mobile app instead of visiting a branch.

The board proposed a cash dividend of 10 percent for shareholders. Bonus shares of 5 percent were also proposed. The dividend will be paid after approval at the annual general meeting.

The bank strengthened its cybersecurity controls. A new fraud monitoring system now checks every card transaction in real time. All staff completed phishing awareness training, and no major security breach was reported.

Employee headcount rose to 2,400 staff. The bank hired 300 graduates for its new branches. Staff turnover fell to 6 percent, and training hours per employee doubled.

Green finance became a new priority. The bank launched green loans for solar panels and electric vehicles. It aims to grow green lending to 5 percent of its loan portfolio within three years.
